# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- New Dvergr crate in the Storage tab (workbench: 10 Wood, 2 Resin): storage built from the dvergr
  crate prop with 3 x 3 slots. Its lid swings open while someone has it open, like a chest's.
- New Hanging surtling core in the Lighting tab (workbench: 1 Surtling core, 2 Leather scraps): a
  core held in a knotted rope that hangs under a beam or roof and glows without fuel. It never goes out,
  but it is not a fire: no warmth, no comfort.

## 0.1.2

- Players on 0.1.x can still play together.

## 0.1.1

- Rune carvings are grey instead of black, and their ember glow grows in a straight line from noon
  to midnight instead of switching on around sunset. The night glow is dimmer than before and
  patchy like embers, slowly shifting.
- New client-side settings MinGlow and MaxGlow set the glow at noon and at midnight.
- Rune carvings cost less to draw: carvings out of view are no longer updated every frame.
- Rune carvings no longer flicker or vanish into the surface they are on when seen at a steep angle.

## 0.1.0

- First release: the Dvergr cask, a Plains-tier fermenter built from the dvergr barrel prop
  (workbench: 30 Fine wood, 5 Black metal, 10 Resin). It ferments every mead the vanilla fermenter can, under
  the same roof rules, in 30 minutes instead of 40 and with 8 meads per batch instead of 6.
- Twelve carved stone idols (Stone idol I to XII) in the hammer's Decor tab (stonecutter: 20 Stone each).
  They are decoration only; Stone idol I's stone bowl is open, so you can place things in it.
- Rune carvings in the Furniture tab (workbench: 1 Coal): runes on a wall, beam or post that
  you write like a sign, in the runestones' rune lettering. One runs left to right in a single line,
  the vertical one top to bottom in a single column. The runes are black by day and glow like
  embers in the dark.
- Names and descriptions are translated into every language Valheim offers except Abenaki, and
  follow the game's language setting.
